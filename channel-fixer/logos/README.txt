Your own channel logos
======================

Put a picture here named after the channel, for example:

    TLC.png
    WPIX.png
    E!.png

The name is matched loosely (capitals, spaces and punctuation don't matter),
against the channel name shown in Channel Fixer. Transparent PNGs look best in
TiviMate. Then click "Load channels" again and "Share all on my home network".

These logos are served to TiviMate by the helper, so start it with --lan.
